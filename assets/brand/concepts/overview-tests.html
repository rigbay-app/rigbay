Rigbay concept tests

One-colour, reversed, squint, mirror / rotate, shelf and family checks. Rendered with headless Edge, unscaled.

1 · One colour: black on white, white on black (every fill one value, so the accent has to survive as shape alone). Order: A, B, C

2 · Reversed on the Rigbay accent (white, one colour) and full colour on Mica light / dark

3 · Squint (blur 3 px at 64 px: light, dark, small cut) and mirror / rotate 180 / rotate 90

4 · Shelf test: each concept (dashed outline) among neighbours at 48 px, light and dark. Neighbours are generic stand-ins for the conventions of PowerToys (bright toolbox tile), MSI Center and Armoury Crate (black and red, angular or round badge) and HWiNFO (dense green chip and graph tile). They are drawn from category habits; they are not those logos.

5 · Family: the Dock app icon with the four module tiles (shared outer radius 56, one accent each), 64 / 32 / 16 px, light and dark
